## 简体中文

修复睡眠恢复后的白字、解锁背景闪色、自动隐藏与全屏下的显示问题；竖向排版重做，新增读数位置设置。

### 修复

- 睡眠 / 休眠恢复后数字变白、看不见（要等一分钟才回黑）
- 解锁瞬间背景闪一下深色
- 睡眠恢复后程序偶尔自己退出
- 任务栏自动隐藏时，鼠标划出后不显示数据，或先出现一块灰底
- 全屏软件在前台时，任务栏滑出后数据闪一下就消失

### 改进

- 竖向任务栏排版重做：去掉冒号，标签与数值成组，整列右对齐
- 新增「位置」：自动 / 托盘侧 / 另一端（Win11 居中布局时可贴左端空地）
- 新增「离边缘距离」滑杆（0~400 px），拖动实时生效

### 其他

- 新增诊断日志 `%TEMP%\pico-tray-life.log`：崩溃、退出原因、资源占用与显示状态时间线

## English

Fixes white text and background flashes after sleep, plus display issues with the auto-hidden taskbar and fullscreen apps; reworked the vertical layout and added a readout position setting.

### Fixes

- Text turned white (invisible) after sleep or hibernate, until the one-minute fallback
- Background flashed dark for a moment on unlock
- The program occasionally exited by itself after resume
- With the taskbar set to auto-hide, hovering the edge showed no data, or a grey block first
- With a fullscreen app in front, the readout flashed once and disappeared when the taskbar slid out

### Improvements

- Vertical taskbar layout reworked: colons removed, label and value grouped, right-aligned
- New "Position" setting: Automatic / Tray side / Other end
- New "Distance from edge" slider (0–400 px), applied live while dragging

### Other

- New diagnostic log at `%TEMP%\pico-tray-life.log`: crashes, exit reason, resource use and show/hide timeline
