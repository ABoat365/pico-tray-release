## 简体中文

修复一个会让程序在电脑进入睡眠的瞬间无声退出的崩溃。

### 修复

- **进入睡眠时进程直接消失**：程序持有一个 WMI 连接（笔记本内置屏的亮度走这条路），它是 COM 代理，所属的 COM 公寓由创建它的后台线程维持。那个线程是短命的——枚举完显示器就退出，公寓随之被拆，代理于是变成野指针（内存已回收，指针还留着）。1.0.3 在收到"即将睡眠"的通知时主动去释放这个代理，正好撞在野指针上，所以每次睡眠都会崩，托盘上的读数跟着消失，日志之外没有任何提示。现在两处都收紧了：主线程在启动时占住一个长期公寓，代理的寿命不再挂在后台线程身上；"即将睡眠"那一刻对代理只丢弃指针、不做任何调用——那时系统已经在拆通信通道，调进去同样不安全。
- 崩溃当场的表现是「电脑睡了一觉，醒来托盘里没有这个小工具了」，事件查看器里能看到 `pico-tray.exe` 的 `0xc0000005`。

### 其他

- 发布流程里加了一道睡眠/唤醒路径的回归测试：向程序发送模拟的睡眠与唤醒通知，检查进程是否存活、读数窗口是否重建。这类"平时不走、只在睡眠时出现"的问题，以后不会再靠运气发现。

## English

Fixes a crash that made the app disappear silently the moment the PC went to sleep.

### Fixes

- **Process vanishing on suspend**: the app holds a WMI connection (used for the built-in
  panel's brightness). It is a COM proxy, and the COM apartment it belongs to is kept alive by
  the background thread that created it. That thread is short-lived — it exits right after
  enumerating monitors, the apartment is torn down, and the proxy is left dangling (memory
  reclaimed, pointer still held). 1.0.3 released that proxy when the "about to suspend"
  notification arrived, landing exactly on the dangling pointer, so every sleep killed the
  process and the taskbar readout disappeared, with nothing but a log line to show for it.
  Two things changed: the main thread now holds a long-lived apartment at startup, so the
  proxy's lifetime no longer depends on a background thread; and at "about to suspend" the
  proxy is only dropped, never called — the system is already tearing down its communication
  channels at that point, so calling in is just as unsafe.
- What you would have seen: the app is simply gone from the tray after the PC wakes up, and
  the event log shows a `0xc0000005` from `pico-tray.exe`.

### Other

- The release process now runs a sleep/wake regression check: it sends the app simulated
  suspend and resume notifications and verifies the process survives and the readout window is
  rebuilt. Problems that only show up around sleep no longer depend on luck to be found.
