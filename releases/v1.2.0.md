## 简体中文

「磁盘脱机 / 联机」换成「磁盘禁用 / 启用」：盘真正停转，也不再被后台程序唤醒。

### 新增

- **磁盘禁用 / 启用**：替掉原来的「磁盘脱机 / 联机」。禁用后盘立刻停转，并从资源管理器、磁盘管理和任务管理器里彻底消失；启用时它自己起转，盘符和分区都会回来。
- 禁用前会检查页面文件、存储池与跨区卷，碰到这三种情况直接拒绝并说明原因。

### 修复

- **设为脱机后机械盘照样被唤醒**：打开任务管理器或资源管理器，盘又转起来，噪音照旧。
- **禁用再启用后盘符不回来**：任务管理器里能看到这块盘，资源管理器里却没有。现在会等盘起转、重新读分区表，盘符和分区自动恢复。
- **提示「这块盘上还有程序开着文件」，却根本找不到是谁**：不再因为盘被挂载着就拒绝禁用。

极端情况下启用后盘符仍可能不回来 —— 重启一次电脑即可恢复；磁盘管理提示「必须初始化」时不要点初始化，那会毁掉数据。

### 安装

下载 `PicoTray-1.2.0-setup.exe` 双击安装，会覆盖旧版本并保留设置。已装 1.1.0 的用户下次启动会收到更新提示。

## English

The disk offline / online panel becomes disk disable / enable: the drive really spins down and
is no longer woken up by background programs.

### New

- **Disable disk / Enable disk** replaces the old "offline / online". A disabled drive spins down
  immediately and disappears from Explorer, Disk Management and Task Manager; enabling spins it
  back up and brings its drive letters and partitions back.
- Before disabling, the page file, storage pools and spanned volumes are checked — if one of them
  lives on the drive, the action is refused with an explanation.

### Fixes

- **An offline drive still being woken up**: opening Task Manager or Explorer got the drive
  spinning again, with the same noise.
- **No drive letter after disable → enable**: the drive showed up in Task Manager but not in
  Explorer. It now spins up and re-reads its partition table, so letters and partitions return.
- **"Files are still open on this drive" with no way to find out what**: a mounted drive is no
  longer treated as a reason to refuse.

In rare cases the drive letter may still not come back right after enabling — restart the
computer to recover. If Disk Management says the disk must be initialized, do not initialize it.

### Installation

Download `PicoTray-1.2.0-setup.exe` and run it; your settings are preserved. Existing 1.1.0
installs will be offered the update on next start.
