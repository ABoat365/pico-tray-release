## 简体中文

卸载程序时，之前被禁用的磁盘会自动恢复，不会再留下一块"消失的盘"。

### 新增

- **卸载时自动恢复磁盘**：程序每次禁用磁盘都会记下那块盘，卸载时逐个启用回来。覆盖安装（升级）不会触发，免得把你特意停着的盘吵醒。

### 安装

下载 `PicoTray-1.2.1-setup.exe` 双击安装，会覆盖旧版本并保留设置。已装 1.2.0 的用户下次启动会收到更新提示。

---

## English

Uninstalling the app now switches any disk it disabled back on, so no drive goes missing.

### New

- **Disks are restored on uninstall**: every disk the app disables is remembered and turned back on when you uninstall. In-place upgrades do not trigger this, so a drive you deliberately keep off stays off.

### Installation

Download `PicoTray-1.2.1-setup.exe` and run it; your settings are preserved. Existing 1.2.0
installs will be offered the update on next start.
