## 简体中文

设置里的网卡列表会跟着系统变化走了。

### 新增

- **展开网卡下拉时自动刷新**：插上 USB 网卡、连 VPN、开关 Wi-Fi 之后，直接展开下拉就能选到，不用关掉设置再打开。

### 修复

- 手动指定的网卡拔掉又被别的网卡顶替后，插回来时会自动切回这块网卡；以前会一直停在备用网卡上。

### 安装

下载 `PicoTray-1.3.2-setup.exe` 双击安装，会覆盖旧版本并保留设置。已装 1.3.x 的用户下次启动会收到更新提示。

---

## English

The adapter list in Settings now follows changes in the system.

### New

- **The adapter dropdown refreshes when you open it**: plug in a USB NIC, connect a VPN or toggle Wi-Fi and the new adapter is right there, no need to reopen Settings.

### Fixed

- If the adapter you picked manually was unplugged and another one took over, it is now picked up again when you plug it back in instead of staying on the fallback adapter.

### Installation

Download `PicoTray-1.3.2-setup.exe` and run it; your settings are preserved. Existing 1.3.x
installs will be offered the update on next start.
